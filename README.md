## Experiment: AI-Powered Retail Customer Support Chatbot

## Aim:

To design and develop an AI-powered chatbot that can handle customer inquiries, provide support, and improve customer experience in a retail environment using various AI prompting techniques.

## Algorithm:
Select an online retail store as the chatbot scenario.
Identify common customer queries such as product availability, delivery, returns, refunds, and complaints.
Prepare different prompts using various prompting techniques.
Provide the prompts and customer queries to an AI chatbot.
Collect and record the chatbot responses.
Analyze the responses based on accuracy, relevance, clarity, helpfulness, and professionalism.
Compare the results obtained from different prompting techniques.
Identify the prompting technique that provides the most effective customer support.
Prepare the final report based on the experimental results.
## Prompt:

1. Zero-Shot Prompting
You are an AI-powered customer support chatbot for an online retail store.
Answer the customer's query clearly, politely, and concisely.

Customer Query:
How can I return a product?

2. Few-Shot Prompting
You are a retail customer support chatbot.

Example 1:
Customer: How long does delivery take?
Chatbot: Standard delivery usually takes 3–5 business days.

Example 2:
Customer: Can I cancel my order?
Chatbot: Yes, you can cancel your order before it is shipped.

Now answer the following query:

Customer: How can I return a product?

3. Role-Based Prompting
Act as a professional customer support executive for an online
electronics retail store.

Be polite, friendly, professional, and helpful.
Give a clear solution and explain the next step.

Customer Query:
My product arrived damaged. What should I do?

4. Context-Based Prompting
You are a customer support chatbot for ABC Electronics.

Store policies:
- Products can be returned within 7 days.
- Damaged products can be replaced within 7 days.
- Refunds are processed within 5 business days.
- Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days.

Answer the customer's query using only the information provided.

Customer Query:
My product arrived damaged. What should I do?

5. Step-by-Step Prompting
Analyze the customer's problem step-by-step and determine the
appropriate support action. Then provide only the final
customer-friendly response.

Customer Query:
I received the wrong product. What should I do?

6. Universal Instruction Prompting
You are an AI-powered customer support chatbot for an online retail store.

Follow these instructions:
1. Be polite and professional.
2. Give short and clear answers.
3. Answer only the customer's question.
4. Do not invent information.
5. If information is unavailable, ask for the required details.
6. Provide a useful next step whenever possible.

Customer Query:
Where is my order?

## Output:
Output 1 – Zero-Shot Prompting

You can return a product within 7 days of delivery. Log in to your account, select the order, and choose the “Return Product” option.

Output 2 – Few-Shot Prompting

You can return a product by selecting the order from your account and choosing the “Return Product” option. Returns are accepted within 7 days of delivery.

Output 3 – Role-Based Prompting

I’m sorry that your product arrived damaged. Please contact our customer support team with your order details and a photo of the damaged product. We will assist you with a replacement or return.

Output 4 – Context-Based Prompting

Since the product arrived damaged, you can request a replacement within 7 days of delivery. Please contact customer support with your order details and evidence of the damage.

Output 5 – Step-by-Step Prompting

We’re sorry that you received the wrong product. Please contact customer support with your order details and a photo of the product received. Our team will assist you with the return and replacement.

Output 6 – Universal Instruction Prompting

Please provide your order number so I can help you check the status of your order.

## Result:

The AI-powered retail customer support chatbot was successfully designed using different prompting techniques. The experiment showed that role-based, context-based, and universal instruction prompts produced more accurate, relevant, professional, and helpful responses. Therefore, effective prompt engineering can improve the performance of AI chatbots and provide a better customer experience in a retail environment.
